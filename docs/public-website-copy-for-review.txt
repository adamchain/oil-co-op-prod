HOMEPAGE — OUR STORY (#story)
================================================================================

Section heading: Our story

Paragraph 1:
  The Co-op began in 1981 in connection with a non-profit; when that
  organization wound down, the founders continued the mission of fair pricing
  for Connecticut residents. Citizen's Oil Co-op was incorporated in June 1992
  to unite heating oil consumers and leverage purchasing power.

Paragraph 2:
  Family-owned and operated, the Co-op has grown to over 3,000 members across
  every town in Connecticut and Rhode Island, with expansion into parts of New
  York and Massachusetts (including much of Worcester, Norfolk, and Bristol
  counties in MA and Westchester County, NY).

Paragraph 3:
  Today the Co-op also negotiates programs beyond oil — propane, bioheat, solar,
  audits, insurance, and more — with the same focus: affordable, quality
  full-service energy and advocacy for members.

HOMEPAGE — MEMBERSHIP (#membership)
================================================================================

Section heading: Membership
Section intro: Fees, delivery, referrals, and what membership does not mean.

--- Costs & fees ---

  • $10 non-refundable application fee.
  • $35 annual membership dues, renewing each year.
  • $25 annual rate for seniors 55+ (also applies to some low-volume propane
    accounts per Co-op policy).

Online signup note:
  If you join through this app, charged amounts may follow your configured
  payment system — confirm current published fees with the office.

--- Delivery ---

  Most participating companies use automatic delivery. Some areas may allow
  will-call (you schedule with a few days' notice) — ask the Co-op what's
  available where you live.

  If you leave the Co-op, you must end delivery arrangements with your oil or
  propane company directly; the Co-op cannot cancel for you. You must also
  cancel membership with the Co-op to stop renewals.

--- Referral program ---

  Refer five new active members and become a lifetime member with no annual
  dues. Individual referrals may qualify for waived dues in an upcoming season
  or promotional raffles (e.g. gift cards) when announced.

--- The Next Step program ---

  Nonprofits and community groups can introduce the Co-op to their members. For
  each person who joins, the Co-op donates $10 back to that organization (paid
  bi-annually when totals exceed $50 per period, otherwise carried forward).
  Next Step and the member referral program cannot be combined on the same
  signup.

Sidebar callout — We're on your side:
  Large membership means better contracts — and a voice when you need help with
  pricing, service contracts, or supplier issues.

Pills/tags:
  Not a fixed-price lock-in
  Full-service suppliers
  Advocacy

--- Heating oil (accordion) ---

  All Co-op heating oil suppliers are full-service. Members typically see
  pricing roughly 40–50¢ below average state posted prices, with competitive
  service contracts and access to budget billing programs (often arranged
  before September 1).

  You're billed by your company with time to pay — and the Co-op can help
  advocate if you have service or pricing questions.

--- Propane (accordion) ---

  Discounted propane pricing; many members receive added benefits such as free
  tank rental. The Co-op forwards your information to a participating supplier,
  who contacts you, performs a safety check, switches equipment as needed, and
  coordinates removal of the old tank.

  Existing Co-op members can often add propane without a second membership fee.
  Many oil suppliers also deliver propane for stacked savings.

--- Bioheat (B20) (accordion) ---

  B20 is 80% low-sulfur No. 2 oil and 20% biodiesel — usable in existing oil
  equipment. Biodiesel in the blend is sourced to meet ASTM standards (e.g.
  Greenleaf Biofuels).

  Environmental highlights often cited for B20 include:
  • Meaningful reductions in CO₂, particulates, and other emissions vs.
    conventional oil.
  • Supports domestic fuel production and reduced reliance on imported oil.

  Bioheat is available in many Connecticut towns; where it isn't, rallying
  interest (e.g. ~10 neighbors) can help the Co-op open a route.

--- Solar energy (accordion) ---

  Solar can reduce purchased electricity with a fixed energy rate. Many
  households save a significant share vs. utility supply costs; federal (and
  sometimes state) incentives apply — ask for current programs.

  A Co-op representative can review your home and usage. Members who enroll
  through the Co-op have at times qualified for a $500 incentive upon project
  completion and activation (verify current offer).

--- Home energy audits (NESE) (accordion) ---

  Partner New England Smart Energy (NESE) offers audits across Connecticut with
  a modest copay (often around $50), including substantial in-home measures
  (historically up to ~$600 of work) and access to rebates on follow-up
  improvements.

  Pairing audits with cleaner fuels like bioheat can cut both bills and carbon
  footprint.

--- Insurance (Bearingstar) (accordion) ---

  Partner Bearingstar Insurance offers member pricing on auto and homeowners
  coverage. Testimonials cite hundreds of dollars in annual savings vs. prior
  carriers.

--- Electricity (accordion) ---

  Status: The Co-op has previously offered electricity programs; as of recent
  updates, there may not be a live electric supply offer while a new supplier
  relationship is pursued. Call or email for the latest.

--- Blue Earth Compost & more (accordion) ---

  The Co-op highlights additional member programs on oilco-op.com — including
  organics/compost and other seasonal offers. This site focuses on core energy
  programs; visit the main site for the full menu.

--- 1. General Information ---

Q: What is Citizen's Oil Co-op?
A: Citizen's Oil Co-op is a membership-based buyers club that helps homeowners
   save money on heating oil, propane, and other home energy services. We
   negotiate competitive pricing with trusted local full-service companies and
   help members find the best fit for their home.

Q: How long has Citizen's Oil Co-op been in business?
A: We've been helping homeowners save on home energy for over 30 years and
   proudly serve more than 5,000 members throughout Connecticut, Rhode Island,
   Westchester County NY, and select communities in Massachusetts.

Q: Do you deliver heating oil or propane?
A: No. We don't deliver fuel or service heating equipment. Instead, we partner
   with trusted local full-service heating oil and propane companies that handle
   deliveries, equipment service, and customer support at a discounted price for
   members.

Q: What is your service area?
A: We proudly serve homeowners throughout:
   • Connecticut – All towns
   • Rhode Island – All towns
   • New York – Westchester County only
   • Select communities in Massachusetts
   If you're unsure whether your town is covered, just contact us—we're happy
   to help.

--- 2. Membership ---

Q: How much does membership cost?
A: Membership is $35 per year ($25 for seniors) with a one-time $10 application
   fee for new accounts and provides access to Co-op pricing along with
   additional member benefits. You can join at any time. Canceling your Co-op
   membership does not terminate your oil or propane account. You must contact
   the company directly to do this.

Q: What does membership include?
A: Membership includes:
   • Access to Co-op negotiated heating oil or propane pricing
   • Electricity supply rate options
   • Free solar consultation through Trinity Solar
   • Discounted Home Energy Audit through New England Smart Energy
   • Free home and auto insurance quote from Bearingstar Insurance
   • Additional seasonal offers and member discounts

Q: Who should join the Co-op?
A: Our members are homeowners who want competitive pricing, dependable service,
   and the peace of mind that comes from working with a trusted local company—
   without spending hours shopping around every year.

--- 3. Pricing ---

Q: How does Co-op pricing work?
A: Co-op pricing is a discounted variable price that changes with the energy
   market. Through our long-term agreements with participating heating oil and
   propane companies, members receive competitive pricing that reflects current
   market conditions.
   If market prices go down, your Co-op price goes down. If market prices
   increase, your Co-op price will adjust accordingly.
   Each morning, we provide our participating companies with that day's Co-op
   price, and members are billed the Co-op price in effect on the date of their
   delivery.

Q: Is the Co-op price fixed?
A: No. Heating oil and propane prices change with the energy market. Your Co-op
   pricing changes as market prices change, but it's based on pricing we've
   negotiated with our participating companies.
   We do work with one oil company that offers a fixed rate for members. Please
   call us or request more information to see if this company covers your town.

Q: How much can I save?
A: Savings vary depending on market conditions and your location, but the
   average Co-op member can save up to $600 a year. (Based on usage of 900
   gallons.)

--- 4. Choosing Your Heating Company ---

Q: How do you decide which company I'll use?
A: We recommend the participating company from our network that's the best
   overall fit based on your location, pricing, service options, and personal
   preferences.

Q: Can I keep my current heating company?
A: If you're already using one of our participating companies, we'll usually
   keep you with that company while applying Co-op pricing to your account.
   Nothing will change with your service other than a better price on your oil
   or propane.

Q: Can I choose a specific company?
A: Yes. If you have a preferred company, we'll do our best to accommodate your
   request whenever possible.

Q: What if my current company isn't part of the Co-op?
A: To take advantage of Co-op pricing you do have to work with one of the
   companies in our network. We'll recommend one of our participating companies
   that serves your area and is the best fit for you. We'll guide you through
   the transition and answer any questions you may have.

Q: Will I still receive full service?
A: Yes. You'll continue to receive the same full-service benefits offered by
   your participating company, including fuel delivery, equipment service,
   emergency support, and other available programs.

Q: Do I have to sign up for automatic delivery?
A: Not always. Some participating companies offer both automatic delivery and
   will-call options, while others require automatic delivery. We'll help you
   find the option that best fits your needs.

Q: Do I have to purchase a service contract?
A: No. Service contracts are optional, although many members choose one for
   added peace of mind and equipment protection. We'll explain your options so
   you can decide what's right for you.

--- 5. Propane and Propane Tanks ---

Q: Do I own my propane tank?
A: It depends. Some homeowners own their propane tank, while others rent one
   from their propane provider.

Q: What's the difference between owning and renting a propane tank?
A: If you own your tank, you're free to choose the propane company that fills
   it. This gives you greater flexibility if you ever decide to switch
   providers.
   If you rent your tank, the tank belongs to your propane company. Because of
   safety and liability requirements, only that company can fill or service its
   own tank.

Q: Is it better to own or rent a propane tank?
A: Both options have advantages.
   Owning your tank:
   • Freedom to choose your propane supplier
   • More flexibility if you decide to switch companies
   • No annual tank rental fee
   • Higher upfront investment
   Renting your tank:
   • Little or no upfront cost
   • The propane company maintains the tank
   • Annual rental fee may apply
   • You'll typically purchase propane from that company while using their tank
   We'll gladly explain the differences and help you understand which option
   best fits your situation.

Q: Can I switch propane companies if I rent my tank?
A: Yes.
   If you decide to switch propane companies, the new company will typically:
   • Perform a safety inspection
   • Remove the existing rental tank
   • Install their own rental tank
   • Transfer your account and service
   If there's propane remaining in the old tank, the previous company is
   required to reimburse you for the unused fuel. The exact process may vary
   slightly by company.

--- 6. Sign me up! What happens next? ---

Q: What happens after I join?
A: After you become a member, we'll review your location and preferences,
   recommend the participating company that's the best fit, and forward your
   information so they can contact you to set up your oil or propane account at
   Co-op pricing.

Q: How long does it take to get started?
A: Most new members hear from their assigned participating company within a day
   or two after joining to complete their account setup. To schedule a first
   delivery for a new account we typically recommend 7–10 days.

Q: Why should I join Citizen's Oil Co-op instead of calling companies myself?
A: We've spent more than 30 years building relationships with trusted heating
   companies and negotiating competitive pricing on behalf of our members.
   Instead of spending your time calling multiple companies, comparing prices,
   and trying to determine who's offering the best value, you can rely on
   Citizen's Oil Co-op to help connect you with a trusted provider that fits
   your needs.

Q: Why do homeowners trust Citizen's Oil Co-op?
A: For over three decades, we've helped thousands of homeowners find competitive
   pricing through trusted local heating companies. Our goal is simple: make
   home energy easier by helping our members receive fair pricing, dependable
   service, and honest guidance.
